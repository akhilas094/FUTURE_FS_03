# Café Luuma — Short Business Pitch

## 60-second pitch

Hello, I’m Akhila, an MCA student. I created this website proposal for Café Luuma to make it easier for customers to discover your café and decide to visit.

The homepage introduces the café, while the menu highlights help people browse their cravings quickly. A customer can find directions, call the café, open your Instagram, or continue to your existing Swiggy and Zomato listings from one place.

The design adapts to mobile screens, so you could share one website link in your Instagram bio or on a printed QR code. It also includes a visit-note planner to help groups prepare their enquiry, while making it clear that availability must be confirmed by phone.

Before launching anything officially, I would like your feedback and approval, along with the correct logo, menu, hours, and café photos. Would you be open to a short demo?

## Three-minute demo walkthrough

1. **Homepage:** Explain the café identity, clear menu button and location hint.
2. **Menu:** Filter by momos or drinks and search for a dish. Explain why current prices link to the existing delivery menus.
3. **Ordering channels:** Show where customers find Swiggy and Zomato links, without placing an order.
4. **Visit section:** Point out the address, Maps search and phone link. Do not initiate a call during the presentation unless the owner requests it.
5. **Visit planner:** Create a sample plan. Explain that it is a local note, not an online reservation.
6. **Owner feedback:** Ask which menu items, photos, opening hours and contact channels the owner wants featured.

## How this can help the business

- Brings essential information into one easy-to-share link.
- Reduces the steps between finding the café and contacting it.
- Gives the café a consistent visual presence beyond delivery apps.
- Helps new visitors browse before making a visit or delivery decision.
- Can support local discovery once approved and publicly hosted.

These are proposed benefits. There are no measured conversion or sales claims.

## Owner review checklist

- [ ] Confirm café name/spelling and approved logo.
- [ ] Confirm phone number and Instagram account.
- [ ] Provide exact Google Maps pin and current opening hours.
- [ ] Approve the menu highlights and decide how prices will be maintained.
- [ ] Supply approved photos of the food and café.
- [ ] Review the design on a real phone.
- [ ] Choose a public website URL/domain and approve launch.
- [ ] Decide whether a future WhatsApp or booking integration is needed.
